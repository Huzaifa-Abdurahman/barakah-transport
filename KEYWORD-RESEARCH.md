## 2. Competitors

Our domain (barakahtransportservice.com) returned **zero ranked keywords** and no competitor overlap in DataForSEO. That matches the audit: the site is not yet indexed beyond the homepage.

### alfatehtransport.com (content-led)

Ranks for ziyarat and mosque content, not only transport. Top keyword set includes:

- masjid al quba (201,000 combined variants), masjid al-qiblatayn (33,100), uhud mountain (49,500), baqi cemetery (22,200), hira cave jabal al-nour (14,800), ghar e hira (5,400), miqat (6,600), miqat near me (4,400)
- transport terms: taxi (8,100), umrah cab, miqat taif

Their blog posts (e.g. the Masjid Quba guide) drive the traffic. Our blog is designed to compete with this approach.

### umrahtaxiservice.com (taxi and pricing)

Ranks for transfer and fare terms, backed by a pricing page:

- taxi (8,100), makkah taxi (1,000), makkah taxi service (720), taxi service in makkah (720)
- jeddah airport to makkah taxi fare (1,000), taxi fare from jeddah airport to makkah (1,000), madinah airport taxi (1,000)
- umrah taxi service (880), umrah taxis (1,000), umrah cab (1,000)

Their advantage is that the fare and transfer terms are on their pages, with prices.

### umrahtaxionline.com (route pages and ziyarat list)

- jeddah to makkah (1,600), jeddah to makkah distance (2,400), ziyarat in makkah (3,600), ziyarat places in makkah (590), makkah ziyarat places list (390)
- madinah to makkah taxi (320), taxi from madinah to makkah (320), jeddah to makkah taxi (260)
- madina ziyarat places (1,300)

Their advantage is a dedicated route page and a ziyarat list page for almost every high-volume ziyarat term.

## 3. What the live search results show

- **"Jeddah airport to Makkah taxi":** the first result is an AI overview that lists fares by vehicle (SAR 180–450). Transfer companies with prices in the snippet rank next. **We have no price on our route pages**, so we are not competing for the price-led click. Confirmed fares are the single biggest gap (see TODO-CLIENT.md, section 4).
- **"Makkah ziyarat places":** the SERP is almost all blogs and travel agencies. There is no strong transport-company guide, so a well-written guide is a realistic target.
- **"Ziyarat places in Madinah":** the local pack shows Masjid Quba, Qiblatain and the Ghars well. The organic results are blogs. Low competition (0.05) makes this a good first target.

## 4. Priorities

1. **Confirm fares** for every route page. This unlocks "from SAR X" in titles and snippets, which the competitors use.
2. **Ziyarat guides** (published in this pass): Makkah ziyarat, Madinah ziyarat, Cave Hira. These cover the highest-volume, lowest-competition informational terms.
3. **Route guides** (published in this pass): Jeddah airport to Makkah (distance and options), Makkah to Madinah (taxi, train, bus), Miqat for Madinah pilgrims.
4. **Umrah taxi checklist** (published in this pass): targets "umrah taxi" and "umrah transport" and links to the fleet and route pages.
5. **Google Business Profile**: needed for the local pack, which is shown for the Madinah ziyarat terms.
6. **Arabic pages** (Phase 5): "نقل معتمرين" and "تاكسي مكة".
7. **Track rankings** once the site is indexed and the search console shows impressions. This report is a baseline; it is not a ranking result.
